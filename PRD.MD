Product Requirement Document (PRD)
Product Name: Arogya Care

Type: AI-powered Preventive Healthcare & Treatment Recommendation Platform
Platform: Web Application

1. Product Overview

Arogya Care is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to analyze patient data and provide personalized treatment recommendations, disease risk prediction, lifestyle simulations, and cost optimization.

The system creates a Digital Health Twin of the patient using medical data and lifestyle information to simulate possible health outcomes and recommend the best treatment and lifestyle improvements.

Goal

Help patients and doctors make data-driven healthcare decisions to improve health outcomes and reduce treatment costs.

2. Target Users

Patients

Monitor health risks

Get lifestyle recommendations

Track health improvements

Doctors

View patient insights

Analyze AI predictions

Recommend treatments

Healthcare Providers

Reduce treatment cost

Prevent disease early

3. Problem Statement

Healthcare systems often focus on treatment after disease occurs rather than prevention.

Problems:

Lack of personalized healthcare insights

No predictive disease analysis

High treatment cost without comparison

No simulation of lifestyle impact

Arogya Care solves this using AI-driven predictive healthcare.
